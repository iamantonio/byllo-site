Your name, mobile phone number, a description of your service request, your zip code, any photos you choose to send, and your SMS consent choice with its date and time.

How we use it

Only to respond to your service request, including sending text messages you have agreed to receive, and to keep records of your consent.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We use an SMS delivery provider only to deliver messages on our behalf; it may not use your information for any other purpose. We may also disclose information if required by law. We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number or SMS opt-in data with anyone for their marketing or promotional purposes.

Text messaging

If you opt in, Antonio Vargas sends follow-up text messages about your service request. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. If you text us first, that is also an opt-in to receive replies about that request.

Retention and choices

We keep request information only as long as needed to handle your request. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or email vargas.tony@gmail.com to ask us to delete your information.
