Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

This policy describes how Antonio Vargas, a sole proprietor in Brookfield, IL ("I," "we"), handles information you submit on this site when you request a demo of Byllo, my text follow-up tool.

Information we collect

Your name, mobile phone number, what you'd like to see in the demo, any replies you text back, and your SMS consent choice with its date and time.

How we use it

Only to respond to your demo request, including sending the text messages you agreed to receive, and to keep a record of your consent.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile number, SMS opt-in data, or consent with anyone.

Text messaging

You opt in only by checking the optional SMS consent box on the demo request form at https://iamantonio.github.io/byllo-site/ and submitting it. If you opt in, Antonio Vargas sends follow-up text messages about your demo request, typically 2–5 messages per request; message frequency varies. No marketing messages are sent. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help.

Retention and choices

We keep demo request information only as long as needed to handle your request. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or email vargas.tony@gmail.com to ask us to delete your information.
