Privacy Policy

Effective October 19, 2026

This policy describes how Antonio Vargas, a sole proprietor in Brookfield, IL ("I," "me"), handles information you submit on this site when you request a demo of Byllo, my text follow-up tool. It is the only privacy policy for this site and for the Antonio Vargas demo text program.

Information I collect

Your name, email address, mobile phone number (if you give one), what you'd like to see in the demo, whether you checked the optional SMS consent box, the date and time you submitted the form, and any replies you text back.

How I use it

Only to respond to your demo request: by email, and by text message if you opted in. I also keep a record of your SMS consent and any opt-out. I don't use your information for marketing, and I don't add you to any mailing or texting list.

Sharing

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

I do not sell, rent, or trade your information, and I do not pass your request or phone number to other businesses. To run this site and send texts, I use service providers that handle your information only on my behalf and only to deliver it: FormSubmit delivers form submissions to my email, Google (Gmail) stores that email, and Twilio delivers text messages. They are not permitted to use it for their own marketing. I may disclose information only if the law requires it.

Text messaging

You opt in only by checking the optional SMS consent box on the demo request form at https://iamantonio.github.io/byllo-site/ and submitting it. If you opt in, Antonio Vargas sends follow-up text messages about your demo request, typically 2–5 messages per request; message frequency varies. No marketing messages are sent. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for help. See the SMS Terms.

Retention and your choices

I keep demo request information only as long as needed to handle your request, plus the record of your consent or opt-out. Reply STOP to any text to opt out, or email vargas.tony@gmail.com to ask me to delete your information.

Changes

If I change this policy, I'll update the effective date above.
